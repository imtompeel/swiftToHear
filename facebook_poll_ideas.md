## Posting Strategy Recommendations:

1. **Start with Option 1** (Scripture-Focused) to gauge interest in the core challenges
2. **Follow with Option 4** (Scripture Memory) to connect with biblical foundations
3. **Use Option 2** (Listening Practice) to introduce the concept of structured listening
4. **End with Option 7** (Ecclesial Life) to tie it all together and drive to the landing page

## Engagement Tips:

- **Post timing:** Tuesday-Thursday, 7-9 PM (when people are more likely to engage)
- **Follow-up posts:** Always include a brief explanation and link to your landing page
- **Respond to comments:** Engage personally with each comment to build community
- **Share results:** Post a follow-up showing poll results and invite further discussion
- **Cross-reference:** Mention how the poll results relate to your platform's features
